datasets/raw/   --  CSV GOC TU KAGGLE (bo The Movies Dataset)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Khong co gi sau buoc lam sach doc toi day. Giu lai chi de luu vet va de chay
lai pipeline tu dau khi can.

                          cua he thong. Day la nguon diem xep hang, KHONG phai
                          diem TMDB.

                          noi dung.

                          vote_average, vote_count.

                          khi diem so lai den tu rating MovieLens.

dung de chay thu src/models/als_model/als.py (ban ALS tu cai, ma tran dense),
khong can tren cloud.
